Privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

Worlds is run by Empirical. This page explains what Worlds stores about you and the worlds you create.

Signing in with Google

When you sign in with Google, Worlds receives your name, email address, profile picture and Google account ID. We use them only to sign you in, to show you your account, and to contact you about it. We do not request access to any other Google data, such as Gmail, Drive or Calendar.

Worlds' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not use it to train AI models.

API keys

API keys you create are shown once and stored only as a hash. We record when each key was created and last used.

What you put in a world

A world stores what you seed it with and what your app and its users do in it: workspaces, users, channels, messages, files, repositories and similar records, plus a journal of every request, change and outbound event. You decide what goes in. If you seed a world with real data, such as recorded conversations, you are responsible for having the right to use it.

Worlds and their snapshots are private to your account. A world and its journal are deleted when it expires (24 hours by default, unless you set a different lifetime) or when you delete it, whichever comes first.

Where it is stored

Everything is stored on Cloudflare. We use a session cookie to keep you signed in. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not sell or share your data.

Deleting your data

You can delete any world or API key yourself. To delete your account and everything in it, email company@empirical.run.
