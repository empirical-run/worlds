01

What a world is

A world holds the state of every service in it: users, channels, messages, installs. It persists between requests, so a test can set up a scene, run your app against it and check what happened.

Services in a world speak the real wire protocol. Your app keeps its SDK and only changes its base URL.

03

How it works

  • Web API and OAuth. Install your app through the world's OAuth flow and call the same methods you call on the real service.
  • Signed events. Mentions and messages arrive at your events URL, signed and retried the way the real service does it.
  • A UI to act in. Post as any user, create channels and add your app, and watch your app reply.
